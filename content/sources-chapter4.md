## 1. Making pits again

- **DL** `gov.uscourts.scd.265178`. *Savannah River Site Watch et al. v. U.S. Department of Energy et al.*, D.S.C. The challenge to the 2020 pit decision. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20241114223725/https://www.srs.gov/general/news/releases/2024/nr24_SRS_partnerships_critical_to_SRPPF_project_completion_by_2032.pdf. SRS release, November 14, 2024. The plant is being built by repurposing an unfinished concrete structure; the contractor says SRS "must deliver the SRPPF project by 2032". PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **IA** `crs-r44442.31`. Congressional Research Service, *Energy and Water Development Appropriations: Nuclear Weapons Activities* (R44442, version 31), March 14, 2022. NNSA expected 50 pits a year at SRS between 2032 and 2035. Full text read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20261025183933/https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-104661. GAO-23-104661, January 12, 2023, with recommendation status updated to April 2026. No complete schedule or life-cycle cost estimate; at least $18 billion to $24 billion in potential costs. Page read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20260226163054/https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-107777.pdf. GAO-26-107777, *Assessments of NNSA Major Projects*, February 2026. The main building's possible cost over $22 billion (preliminary $6.9 billion to $11.1 billion); completion September 2035. PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20260308145100/https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/SRS%20FY%202025%20Performance%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf. NNSA performance evaluation of Savannah River Nuclear Solutions for fiscal 2025, December 9, 2025. "Underperformed" on the pit plant; "negligent in reporting project slips"; dilute-and-dispose and shipment targets exceeded. PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20261016212506/https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/nnsa-baselines-savannah-river-plutonium-processing-facility. NNSA release, October 16, 2026. Baseline signed and full construction authorized October 15, 2026. No cost or completion date given. Page read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20230913230207/https://www.dnfsb.gov/sites/default/files/document/28871/SRPPF%20Facility%20Worker%20Protection%20[2023-100-025].pdf. Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board letter, August 3, 2023: planned controls "may not adequately protect facility workers". PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20261107181328/https://ehss.energy.gov/deprep/2025/FB25Y21A.PDF. Safety board letter, May 21, 2025: the upgrades are "a significant improvement to safety". No Wayback capture. PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07. Wayback capture of https://ehss.energy.gov/deprep/2025/FB25Y21A.PDF (held by DOE EHSS).

## 2. Moving plutonium out

- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20200902214336/https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-brouillette-and-south-carolina-officials-announce-historic-agreement-between. DOE release, August 31, 2020. $600 million to South Carolina; 9.5 metric tons to be removed by 2037; 2049 otherwise. Page read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20241127184933/https://www.srs.gov/general/news/releases/2024/nr24_The_Future_is_Now_downblended_plutonium_shipping_package_receipt.pdf. SRS release, July 11, 2024. How dilute and dispose works; tens of thousands of drums to WIPP over three decades. PDF read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20251104001902/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-05-29/html/2025-09801.htm. Executive Order 14302, *Federal Register*, May 29, 2025. Halt dilute and dispose except for obligations to South Carolina; offer surplus plutonium to industry. Text read. Verified 2026-11-07.
- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20260311220728/https://www.ans.org/news/2026-03-11/article-7836/return-of-the-hb-line-at-srs. American Nuclear Society *Nuclear Newswire*, March 11, 2026 (trade press quoting DOE). HB Line to restart to recycle plutonium into fuel; disposition 10 to 13 years faster. Page read. Verified 2026-11-07.

- **WB** https://web.archive.org/web/20260706062813/https://www.energy.gov/nepa/articles/doeeis-0279-amended-record-decision-february-2026. DOE amended Record of Decision, February 2026, restoring uranium recovery at H Canyon. Page read. Verified 2026-11-07.

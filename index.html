Atomic History: The Savannah River Story

The Savannah River Plant in South Carolina made plutonium and tritium for American nuclear weapons from 1953 until 1988, and is now cleaning up that legacy while gearing up for new weapons work. Its story, from the people moved off the land to what is happening now, is told here through resources held in Democracy's Library, the Wayback Machine and Internet Archive collections.

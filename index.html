Bomb Making in South Carolina: The Savannah River Plant

The Savannah River Plant in South Carolina made plutonium and tritium for American nuclear weapons from 1953 until 1988. This is its history, from the towns moved off the land to the clean-up and what's happening now, told from records held in Democracy's Library, the wider Internet Archive, the Wayback Machine and elsewhere.

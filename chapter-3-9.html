The Laboratories

The site's laboratory was named the country's newest national laboratory in 2004 (WB). The ecology laboratory fared worse. Its grant was cut 47 percent for 2006, with layoffs that September (WB: UGA); the next year's funding fell to $1 million against an expected $4.5 million, which the site's own Citizens Advisory Board called reprehensible (WB: SRS CAB).

The House Science Committee held a hearing on it in July 2007 (DL; video IA). SREL's (Savannah River Ecology Laboratory's) web page from that summer mentions a statement on a budget crisis (WB) but the actual statement has been lost.

By the 2000s the site's own history had become something to preserve, the subject of The Plant's Museum.
